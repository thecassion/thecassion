## 👨🏾‍💻 About Me

- 🏢 **IT Manager & Principal Software Engineer** at [Caris Foundation International](https://www.carisfoundationintl.org/), where I lead the HMIS platform: cloud infrastructure on AWS, backend services, mobile apps and data pipelines supporting health programs in Haiti.
- 🌍 **Project Analyst Consultant** at [Dimagi](https://www.dimagi.com/), designing and deploying solutions on the **CommCare** platform.
- 🎓 **Lecturer** at Université d'État d'Haïti (Campus Limonade) and Vice-President for Training, Research & Outreach at the AAFDS alumni association.
- 📚 Completing a **Master 2 MIAGE – MBDS** (Mobiquity, Big Data & Systems Integration) at FDS-UEH in partnership with **Université Côte d'Azur**.

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- 🏥 **Digital health systems:** EHR/HMIS platforms, CommCare integrations and webhook bridges, offline-first mobile apps for field workers
- 🤖 **Conversational AI:** multi-channel, multilingual chatbots built with **LangGraph** and **LangChain**
- 🧠 **Medical imaging:** segmentation pipelines with **nnU-Net**
- 📊 **Data engineering & analytics** for program monitoring and evaluation
- 📱 **EdTech:** learning tools for Haitian students based on past exams, quizzes and AI explanations

## 🌱 Currently Exploring
